# Sample University Academic Policy Guide

## Graduation Requirements

Students must complete a minimum of 120 credit hours to graduate. At least 30 credit hours must be completed at the institution. Students must maintain a cumulative GPA of 2.0 or higher and submit a graduation application before the published deadline.

## Attendance Policy

Students are expected to attend all scheduled classes. Instructors may set course-specific attendance expectations. Students who miss classes because of documented medical, family, or university-related reasons should notify the instructor as soon as possible.

## Course Prerequisites

Students must complete required prerequisite courses before enrolling in advanced courses. Prerequisite requirements are listed in the course catalog. Students should consult an academic advisor if they need assistance planning their course sequence.

## Academic Support

Academic advising, tutoring, writing support, and career services are available to enrolled students. Students can contact the student-support office to learn about available services and appointment scheduling.
